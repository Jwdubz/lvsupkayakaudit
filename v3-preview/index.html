What I found

01

Your homepage loads about 12 MB, and the Emerald Cave page about 13 MB.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, your homepage loaded 12.3 MB and its first content showed at 2.2 seconds, but its largest part, the rotating photo at the top, didn't finish until 24.6 and 24.7 seconds (speed score 55 on both runs). Two photos make most of the weight: Rental-Without-Transportation-image-2-2.jpg is about 4.2 MB and image-4-2.jpg about 2.7 MB. The test estimates that sizing the images properly would save about 7 MB. Your Emerald Cave Kayak Tour page loaded 13.3 MB, its largest part finished at 46.0 seconds and its speed score was 39. Your Media page loaded 7.7 MB (score 59). One test setup; field speed is unmeasured.

02

I loaded your homepage, the Emerald Cave tour page and the Hoover Dam tour page in a browser and scrolled each to the bottom. None has a video on it. What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt blocks /edit/ and a list of other crawlers (MegaIndex, Baiduspider, Yandex and similar), and it names your sitemap. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML contains about 870 words of text and a heading, “LAS VEGAS SUP KAYAK CLUB”, so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read your tours and prices. A second, empty heading sits above it.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

  • Found
    Business markup has the basics

    Your page code describes a local business with its name, phone, price range, Henderson address and map position, and it carries FAQ markup on the FAQ page.

  • Gap
    No hours and no social profiles in the markup

    The opening-hours list is empty and the markup lists no social profiles. The five tour pages carry only breadcrumb markup, so the prices and durations on the cards aren't in the page code.

  • Gap
    Reviews load from a widget script

    The “What our guests say” section is drawn by an Elfsight script. No page I opened carries a rating or review in its page code, so a tool that reads only the code can't see them.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.
